LEGAL & COMPLIANCE

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Revised: August 13, 2026

1. Information We Collect

TechPrep AI collects student and administrator data required to provide Data Structures & Algorithms (DSA) training, ATS resume scoring, and campus placement tracking:

  • Account Identity Information: Name, email address, password hashes, contact numbers, and profile avatars.
  • Academic Credentials: College name, degree, department branch, CGPA, 10th/12th percentages, graduation year, and backlog status.
  • Technical Telemetry: Quiz attempts, completion timestamps, score metrics, code editor execution outputs, and proctoring tab-switch warnings.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use collected information solely for educational and placement preparation purposes:

  • Calculating student placement readiness scores and generating skill analytics radars.
  • Performing ATS keyword matching and parsing feedback against target Software Engineer job descriptions.
  • Displaying student performance telemetry to authorized college Training & Placement Officers (TPOs) and TechPrep AI administrators.
  • Maintaining exam security through tab-switch infraction counters during live assessments.

3. Data Security & Encryption

We enforce strict security safeguards to protect your personal and academic information:

  • All student resumes and code submissions are stored securely and never sold to third-party ad networks.
  • Local browser data (localStorage) is managed safely and can be exported or cleared by administrators at any time.
  • Passwords are stored using secure salted hash digests.

4. Your Rights & Data Export

Students and administrators have full control over their registered profile data:

  • You may update or complete your academic profile details anytime from your Student Portal dashboard.
  • Administrators can export a full JSON database backup of all site data via the Site Management console.
  • You may request complete account deletion by contacting privacy@techprep.ai.
